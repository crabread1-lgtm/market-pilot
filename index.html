Learn

Short lessons to build investing fundamentals.
1. What is a stock?

A stock represents a small ownership share in a company. Its price can rise or fall as expectations and market conditions change.

2. Risk and diversification

Diversification spreads investments across assets. It can reduce concentration risk, but cannot eliminate market losses.

3. Market vs. limit orders

A market order prioritizes execution. A limit order sets the maximum buy price or minimum sell price you accept.

4. Long-term thinking

A plan, time horizon, and risk tolerance matter. Avoid decisions based only on short-term price moves.
