MARKETPILOT — PERSONAL IPHONE PAPER-TRADING APP

Open the published GitHub Pages website in Safari on your iPhone. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Includes $25,000 virtual starting cash, simulated buy/sell trades, portfolio holdings, P/L, trade history, lessons, XP and achievements. Progress is stored locally in this browser on this device.

IMPORTANT: Prices are fictional demo values, not live or delayed market quotes. No real money or brokerage account is connected. The website and source repository are public. Do not enter passwords, financial account details, or other sensitive information.
